GoFCards v1.0 — data/gofcards/gofcards_validation_final.csv
=============================================================

Download the full dataset directly:

  Option A — All GOF mutations (Excel, ~easiest):
    http://download.genemed.tech/upload/GainFunCards/gofcards_data_download.xlsx
    Convert ABCC8 + KCNJ11 rows to CSV and save as:
      data/gofcards/gofcards_validation_final.csv

  Option B — Full annotations (ZIP with extended fields):
    http://download.genemed.tech/upload/GainFunCards/gofcards_annotations_download.zip
    Extract and filter ABCC8 + KCNJ11 rows.

  Option C — Browse/export via web UI:
    http://www.genemed.tech/gofcards/#/genemed/download

Required columns in the CSV:
  gene, protein, pscore, am_score, revel_score, phenotype, pmid

Reference: Zhao W et al. GoFCards. Nucleic Acids Res. 2024.
DOI: 10.1093/nar/gkae1079
