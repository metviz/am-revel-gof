# Reproducing the AM/REVEL analysis and manuscript (analysis only)

below. This file lists them in dependency order with their exact inputs and outputs,
so the chain can be re-run from scratch or audited a step at a time.

Run everything from the project root with the venv:

```bash
cd /home/raghu/tools/am_revel_project
/home/raghu/tools/.venv/bin/python scripts/<name>.py
```

`scripts/run_all.py` executes the whole chain in order and stops at the first failure.

| Runtime | ~21 s warm; a few minutes cold (gnomAD pull) |

Caching is asymmetric and worth knowing: the gnomAD pull **is** cached
(`data/gnomad/gnomad_missense_raw.csv` is reused if present), but the ClinVar REVEL
annotation is **not** — re-running always re-queries myvariant.info for 260 variants.

Two details that cost a debugging cycle and are easy to reintroduce:

- `clinvar_processed.csv` is **GRCh38** while every other dataset is GRCh37. The
  myvariant query must pass `assembly="hg38"` for ClinVar and `hg19` for gnomAD.
  Querying ClinVar on hg19 returns 12/260 instead of 219/260.
- The benign proxy is gnomAD missense at AF ≥1e-5 with any ClinVar P/LP assertion
  removed (112 dropped), giving n=425. AM covers 376 of them, REVEL 423 — the
  denominators differ by tool and are reported per row in Table 3.

What each produces, in one line:

- **14** — detection rates at both threshold schemes (author-derived and Bergquist
  2025); LoF-sensitivity-matched operating points; the paired cluster bootstrap for
  δ = gap(AM) − gap(REVEL); LR⁺ with CIs and per-cutoff ceilings against three
  control sets; the DMS complete-case check; BH adjustment over 54 tests.
- **15** — ten dbNSFP predictors at matched LoF sensitivity, grouped by inductive
  bias, with per-dataset and pooled gaps and a usability screen.
- **16** — leave-one-out positional AM prior for all three genes (with ABCC8 isoform

- **17** — the three round-3 statistics: the Strong-threshold shortfall test (CI plus

  Strong cutoffs), panel group means with cell counts and leave-one-cell-out ranges,
  and the ABCC8 isoform tie-break sensitivity. Runtime ~3 s.

## 7. Two bugs worth not reintroducing

Both were silent — they produced well-formed output and were only caught by
verification against source.

**HGVS normalisation.** `re.sub(r"[p\.\(\)]", "", s)` removes *every* letter `p`, so
`p.Asp209Glu` → `As209Glu`. It mangled all Asp and Trp variants (23 of 131). Joins
still "worked" wherever both sides were mangled identically, which is exactly why it
survived. Strip only a leading `p.` prefix. Guarded by `test_tier4.py`.

**ABCC8 isoform numbering.** The cohort numbers ABCC8 one residue higher than UniProt
Q09428 above roughly residue 600. Matching on position alone read 20 of 47 ABCC8
variants at the wrong residue — silently, because a site mean exists at every
position. `16_positional_and_dynamics.py` now matches each variant to the offset whose
cache wild-type residue agrees with its own, and drops anything that matches at
neither. Guarded by `test_tier4.py`.

## 8. Verified reproduction

The analysis chain was re-run end to end and its outputs compared against the
originals. Measured runtimes with warm caches: 10c ~3 s, 13 ~21 s, 14 ~20 s,
15 ~31 s, 16 ~2 s, 17 ~3 s.

relative difference 1.9e-16) from a CSV round-trip. Allele counts and totals are

tier2_lr_table.csv is unchanged. Compare numerically with a tolerance rather than
byte-diffing.
